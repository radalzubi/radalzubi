#### What I'm building

**[ZAAD](https://zaadsupply.com)** (Co-Founder): a B2B decision platform for food and CPG
distributors in MENA. It decides what to buy, hold and promise to customers under uncertain
demand and supply.
- Two-stage stochastic MILP (Pyomo + HiGHS) covering multi-echelon inventory, alternate
  sourcing, open-PO amendments, available-to-promise and perishability.
- Probabilistic forecasting (quantile gradient boosting with conformal calibration) and
  copula-based disruption scenarios.
- 15–30% lower modeled decision cost than baseline planning, tested on real public datasets.
- Python, FastAPI, DuckDB and Next.js, with CI that gates every merge on tests, type
  checks, security scans and a frozen regression that must reproduce recorded decisions exactly.

**Wasel**: a zero-commission ride-hailing marketplace for Jordan, where drivers keep 100% of
fares and pay a flat monthly subscription.
- [Wasel-MVP](https://github.com/radalzubi/Wasel-MVP): Expo rider and driver apps with a
  TypeScript dispatch backend, shipped to TestFlight.
- [wasel-prototype](https://github.com/radalzubi/wasel-prototype): the v1 platform, including
  a weighted driver-matching engine (proximity, subscription tier, reliability, idle time).
- [wasel-sim](https://github.com/radalzubi/wasel-sim): simulation of matching, utilization,
  driver income, churn and unit economics.
